Sovereign Intelligence

A Strategic Framework for Enterprise AI in West Africa

Region: Nigeria

Navigating the intersection of high business maturity and infrastructure constraints. This framework proposes the Secure AI Vault (SAIV) to transform Generative AI from a novelty into a critical dynamic capability.

The Nigerian Paradox

Nigeria presents a unique contradiction. Sectors like FinTech and Energy operate at global standards of governance (CBN, NDPA), creating high demand for advanced analytics. However, the "physics" of AI—power, cooling, and connectivity—lag behind.

Blind adoption of Silicon Valley models creates unacceptable latency and sovereignty risks. We must visualize this gap to address it.

Key Insight

The gap between Regulatory Demand and Infrastructure Readiness is the primary failure mode for standard AI deployments in Lagos.

Market Readiness Assessment

The Cost of Inaction

Without a coherent strategy, the environment breeds "Shadow AI" and data leakage. Innovation occurs in uneven pockets ("Jagged Adoption"), and institutional wisdom remains trapped in silos.

Shadow AI & Data Leakage (40%)

Employees pasting proprietary financial models and contracts into public US-hosted LLMs, breaching NDPA.

Jagged Adoption (25%)

Advanced tools used in HQ while branches rely on manual processes, creating governance blind spots.

Innovation Silos (20%)

Institutional wisdom trapped in senior personnel minds. Capital leaves when they retire.

Missed Dividend (15%)

Failure to capture and reuse successful prompts/workflows for compounding value.
